Parts Hunter

Rare car parts, hunted on eBay by part number

Enter the part number you're looking for. Parts Hunter keeps searching eBay in Germany, the UK, Italy, France and the US, and sends you an alert when a matching listing appears.

Pro

Free watches one part in one country, checked once a day. Pro watches up to 20, in all five countries, checked every 6 hours.

Pro renews until you cancel it. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On a new phone, tap Restore purchase on the Pro screen. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Alerts not arriving?

  • In the app: Settings → New match alerts is on.
  • In the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Parts Hunter, notifications are allowed.
  • Quiet hours hold alerts until they end.
  • A new listing reaches you at the next check: within a day on Free, within 6 hours on Pro.

About matches

A listing matches when your part number appears in its title or details. Sellers often add other part numbers, and a match doesn't prove the part fits your car, so check the number, condition and fit with the seller before you buy. Tap Not the right part to hide a wrong match; it also helps us improve matching.
