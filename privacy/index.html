Privacy in Parts Hunter

Parts Hunter · effective 25 November 2026 · Support · Terms

Parts Hunter watches eBay for the car parts you're hunting and alerts you when one is listed. It keeps only what running your hunts and alerts needs: no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data. This is everything it keeps, and why.

Your account

You sign in with Apple. We keep an account ID and the email address Apple shares with us. If you choose Hide My Email, that's a private relay address and we never see your real one. The account keeps your hunts, cars and settings together, and lets us reach you about your account if we must.

Hunts, cars and settings

  • Hunts: part numbers, the names you give them, max price, condition and the countries to search, so we can search eBay for you and decide which listings match.
  • Cars (only if you add one): make, model, year, engine and a VIN if you enter it, to show which listings fit.
  • Settings: ship-to country, display currency, alerts and quiet hours, to show prices and shipping for you and send alerts when you want them.
  • Matches: which listings you've seen and which you marked "Not the right part", so you don't see a listing twice and we can tell real matches from false ones better.

We don't collect your location (the ship-to country you pick is all we use), contacts, photos, or anything you do outside the app, and we don't use advertising identifiers.

Alerts

iOS asks before Parts Hunter can send notifications. If you allow them, we keep the push token Apple gives this app on this phone, so alerts reach you. You can turn alerts off in the app's settings or in the iPhone's.

eBay

To find listings, we send eBay's official API the part numbers you hunt: search terms only, never your name, email or account. The listings you see come from eBay sellers, and we delete them once they end on eBay.

When you tap Open on eBay, you leave Parts Hunter and eBay's own privacy notice applies. The link may be an eBay Partner Network link, so eBay can tell a purchase came through us and pay us a small commission; we don't add your name or email to it.

Part numbers and the names people give their hunts can help suggest a name when someone else enters the same number. We use only the number and the name, never who entered them.

Pro

You buy Pro through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, receipts and refunds; we never see your card details or your Apple ID. Apple tells our server when you buy Pro, when a subscription renews or ends, and when a purchase is refunded, using an ID that links the purchase to your Parts Hunter account, so Pro works wherever you sign in. We keep whether you have Pro, whether it's monthly or paid once, and when a subscription renews or ends.

Where it's kept, and for how long

Your account and everything in it is stored by Supabase, which runs our database, sign-in and server, in the European Union (Frankfurt). Apple sends notifications and handles sign-in and purchases. They process data only for us.

  • Your account and its data: until you delete your account.
  • Listings from eBay: only while they're live on eBay.
  • Push tokens: until Apple tells us a token no longer works, or you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app's settings, Delete account removes your account, hunts, cars, settings, matches and push tokens at once, and from our backups within 30 days. Without the app, write to the address below; we'll delete it within 30 days and let you know.

Children

Parts Hunter is not made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has sent us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights (EU, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, deleted or restricted, to take it with you, and to object to its processing. Write to us and we'll answer within a month.

We process your account, hunts, cars, settings and alerts so the service you signed up for works (performance of a contract), and use "Not the right part" to improve matching and keep the service secure on the basis of legitimate interest.

Parts Hunter is provided by its developer, the seller named on the App Store. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country; in Croatia that is the Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr).
